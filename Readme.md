# Sales Performance & Profitability Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project explores sales performance and profitability using an interactive Power BI dashboard. It aims to help business stakeholders understand overall sales performance and compare profitability across customer segments.

## Objectives
- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Compare profitability across customer segments.
- Identify potential areas for further investigation.
- Present business metrics through interactive visualizations.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Power BI
- Data visualization and business analysis

## Key Observations
- The dashboard highlights overall sales and profit performance.
- Profitability varies across customer segments, indicating a potential trade-off that requires further investigation.
- Further analysis of seasonal trends, product performance, and the factors affecting segment profitability could provide additional business insights.

## Business Value
The dashboard provides a starting point for understanding sales performance and identifying areas that may require further analysis before making business decisions.
